Code for the Reinforcement Learning Contracted Quantum Eigensolver (RL-CQE) is presented in the accompanying Jupyter notebook.  Research is presented in the manuscript "Quantum Many-body Simulations from a Reinforcement-Learned Exponential Ansatz" by Yuchen Wang and David A. Mazziotti that develops a reinforcement learning approach to solving the contracted Schrodinger equation (CSE) for molecular many-body states on quantum devices.
